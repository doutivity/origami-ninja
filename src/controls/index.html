Controls

Origami Ninja works with a keyboard and on touch screens.

Keyboard

ActionKeys
Move← → or A D
JumpSpace (hold for a higher jump)
GlideHold Space while falling
Tilt the glider↑ slower and longer, ↓ faster and lower
Frog leapHold Space on the ground to crouch and charge, release to leap (a quick tap is a normal jump)
AttackJ
SailHold J while in the boat (Paper Sea): the sail drives you forward against the current
DashDouble-tap J (Kirigami Workshop): a short dash that cuts thick paper walls and flies through arrows
Turtle shellHold J on the ground (Ink Marsh): the shell takes one hit and ignores ink
RestartR

Touch

Four on-screen buttons appear on touch devices: ← and → to move, Jump and Attack. Multi-touch works, so you can run, jump and swing at the same time. Hold Jump in the air to glide, or hold it on the ground to charge a frog leap. Hold Attack on the ground to fold into a turtle shell, double-tap it to dash, or hold it in a boat to raise the sail. Rotate your phone to landscape for the best view.

Tips

  • Fire hurts quickly: you have about three seconds to leave the flames.
  • You get one second of invulnerability after taking damage.
  • The gap in the level cannot be jumped. Fold into a plane and use the wind.
  • Tap or press R on the game over screen to respawn at the last checkpoint.
