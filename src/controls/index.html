Controls

Origami Ninja works with a keyboard and on touch screens.

Keyboard

ActionKeys
Move← → or A D
JumpSpace (hold for a higher jump)
GlideHold Space while falling
Tilt the glider↑ slower and longer, ↓ faster and lower
AttackJ
RestartR

Touch

Four on-screen buttons appear on touch devices: ← and → to move, Jump and Attack. Multi-touch works, so you can run, jump and swing at the same time. Hold Jump in the air to glide. Rotate your phone to landscape for the best view.

Tips

  • Fire hurts quickly: you have about three seconds to leave the flames.
  • You get one second of invulnerability after taking damage.
  • The gap in the level cannot be jumped. Fold into a plane and use the wind.
  • Tap or press R on the game over screen to respawn at the last checkpoint.
