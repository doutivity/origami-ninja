Run. Fold. Glide.

Origami Ninja is a free browser platformer. A paper ninja with a sword races across a world that is one big sheet of paper, and someone is slowly folding it.

Play Controls

Run and slash

Variable-height jumps, coyote time and a sword you can swing on the ground and in the air.

Burn or glide

Fire slowly chars your paper. Hold jump while falling to fold into a paper plane and cross the gap.

Drawn in code

No image files: every shape, flame and icon is generated from one palette, light or dark.
