About Origami Ninja

A paper ninja runs across a world that is one big sheet of paper. Somebody is slowly folding it, and the only way out is towards the centre.

Story

The ninja is made of paper, and so is everything around them. Fire burns the edges, water soaks the fibres, and the sheet keeps folding. The goal of the full game is to reach the centre of the sheet before the folds close in.

Mechanics

  • Run and jump with variable jump height, coyote time and an input buffer.
  • Sword: attack on the ground or in the air. Paper enemies patrol between two points and fall after two hits.
  • Fire takes about 35 HP per second and chars the paper while you stand in it.
  • Glider: hold jump while falling to fold into a paper plane. Tilt it up or down, and ride rising wind.
  • Boat: paper cannot swim. Touch water and the ninja folds into a boat that drifts with the current. Jump, or push into the bank, to climb out.
  • Frog: hold jump on the ground to fold into a frog and charge a leap that clears ledges a normal jump cannot reach.
  • The rising fire: in the Burning Pagoda a wall of flame creeps up from below, so stopping for long is not an option.
  • Hazards: archers shoot fire arrows (a sword swing knocks them down) and burning beams drop from the rafters after a flicker of warning.

Technology

The game is written in TypeScript with Phaser and Arcade Physics, bundled by Vite. Content pages are plain static HTML with no framework; Phaser loads only on the Play page. All graphics are drawn in code, and the whole project builds and runs in Docker.

Plans

The game now has three levels: the mountain, the bamboo river and the burning pagoda. Next come more origami transformations, the folding sheet itself and the goal of reaching the centre. Follow progress in the devlog.
