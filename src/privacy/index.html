Privacy policy

Short version: Origami Ninja does not track you unless you say yes.

What the site stores

Your choices on this site (colour theme and your analytics consent) are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device. The game has no accounts and no saved progress.

Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

If analytics is enabled on this deployment, the site asks for consent in a banner. Until you press Accept, no script from Google is loaded, no request is sent to Google and no cookies are set. Consent Mode v2 is used and advertising storage always stays denied.

If you accept, Google Analytics 4 (and, if configured, Google Tag Manager) measures anonymous usage: page views and a few game events (level start, death and its cause, checkpoint, level complete, quality downgrade). It sets cookies whose names start with _ga. The data helps to understand which parts of the game are too hard or too slow.

Withdrawing consent

Use the “Cookie settings” link in the footer at any time and choose Decline. Analytics is switched off and the _ga cookies are removed. You can also clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Fonts and third parties

Fonts are hosted on this site; the pages make no requests to Google Fonts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log requests under its own privacy policy.
